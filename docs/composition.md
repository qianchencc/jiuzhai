# Jiuzhai — composition before scenery

## Concept
A tactile, restrained cream-paper island with two elevated rear valleys and one foreground valley. Broad water surfaces are the first read; foliage is grouped as framing. The composition is an artistic schematic of real relationships, not a navigation map or a faithful scale model.

## Layers
1. Cut-paper base, horizontally oval and irregular, three pink/cream cardstock strata.
2. Back mountain silhouette: high center-left, lower at the outer shoulders; ivory snow with muted sage-gray folds.
3. Left branch: Changhai at the back and a separate small Wucai pool below. No surface river exits Changhai.
4. Right branch: Wuhua hero lake, Pearl Shoal fall, Mirror Lake.
5. Middle: a broad low Nuorilang paper-ribbon waterfall.
6. Foreground: three to five shallow Shuzheng water terraces, a small Tibetan village to the side.
7. A continuous grounded boardwalk, a small paper traveller, clustered paper forest and a single current-place inscription.

## Palette

## Interaction
The lower-page timeline is the journey itself. Camera positions and gaze move continuously between composed views instead of opening destination cards. The traveller is the scale reference and a direct control along the route. Drag elsewhere to freely orbit; wheel in journey mode moves along water, wheel in free mode changes distance.

Water reacts to a tap and a longer hold. A brush through two foreground forest groves releases folded leaves. The paper sun can be dragged from day to dusk. Sound starts only after an explicit click. Notes and photographs become actual paper leaves beside the boardwalk. Writing is an explicit bottom strip; viewing a saved leaf moves the camera into the landscape.

The making-of page is a separate full-screen paper section, not a scenic detail overlay.

## Revision after critique

The first version's stacked platforms, isolated cones and oval lakes were replaced by one heightfield with folded ridges and genuinely carved basins. Three narrow cut edges belong only to the outside of the island. Shore heights meet the water plane; the four falling-water surfaces and terrain grooves share exact parameters. The upper path ends at a lake shoulder rather than climbing the snow ridge.

There are 380 trees grouped into forest masses with varied needles and folded autumn fans, undergrowth, courtyard houses, stairs and real boardwalk supports. Color stays in the landscape: sage ground and gray-green mountain shoulders, turquoise focal water, cream snow and a warm paper surround. Offline visibility samples supply contact shadows without replacing the live three-dimensional scene.
